2. SERVICE DESCRIPTION

2.1 What Money Mastery Provides

Money Mastery is a cloud-based financial management platform built on Google Apps Script that provides:

  • Transaction Tracking: Import, categorize, and manage income and expense transactions
  • Bank Account Integration: Connect bank accounts via Plaid for automatic transaction synchronization
  • Financial Analytics: Generate reports, charts, and spending insights using BigQuery
  • Clarity AI: AI-powered financial coaching and personalized recommendations
  • Document Management: Store receipts, invoices, and tax documents in Google Cloud Storage
  • Automated Backups: Daily encrypted backups of all financial data
  • Dashboard Interface: Visual financial dashboard with customizable widgets and charts

Money Mastery is provided "as is" and is subject to the following limitations:

  • Google Dependency: Service requires active Google Workspace account; disruptions to Google services may affect availability
  • Plaid Dependency: Bank synchronization depends on Plaid's uptime and bank compatibility
  • Internet Requirement: Service is cloud-based and requires stable internet connection
  • Browser Compatibility: Optimized for modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge); older browsers may experience issues
  • Data Limits: Google Sheets has row limits (~5 million cells per sheet); extremely high transaction volumes may require data archival
