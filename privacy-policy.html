5. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

5.1 Technical Security Controls

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Encryption at Rest: All financial data stored in Google Cloud Storage and BigQuery is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • OAuth 2.0 Authentication: Secure authentication via Google OAuth prevents password storage and reduces credential theft risks
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls limit who can view or modify your data
  • Automated Backups: Daily encrypted backups ensure data recovery in case of system failure

5.2 Organizational Security Measures

  • Limited Employee Access: Only authorized personnel with legitimate business needs can access user data
  • Confidentiality Agreements: All employees and contractors sign non-disclosure agreements
  • Security Training: Regular training on data protection best practices and security protocols
  • Incident Response Plan: Documented procedures for responding to data breaches and security incidents

5.3 Third-Party Security

  • Vendor Assessments: All third-party service providers (Google, Plaid) undergo security reviews
  • Data Processing Agreements: Contractual obligations requiring vendors to protect your data
  • Regular Audits: Periodic reviews of third-party security practices and compliance certifications

5.4 User Security Responsibilities

YOU ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR:

  • Maintaining the confidentiality of your Google account credentials
  • Using strong, unique passwords and enabling two-factor authentication
  • Logging out of shared or public computers
  • Monitoring your account for unauthorized access
  • Reporting suspected security breaches immediately to support@risingandthriving.com

5.5 Limitations of Security

Despite our best efforts, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. You acknowledge and accept this inherent risk when using Money Mastery.

6.2 License Expiration

When your Money Mastery license expires on December 31:

  • YOU LOSE ACCESS TO: Money Mastery sheets, new data imports, Group Support Calls
  • YOU RETAIN ACCESS TO: The Collective community, coaching services, previously downloaded reports
  • YOUR DATA: Remains stored in Google Sheets, BigQuery, and Cloud Storage but is inaccessible until renewal

Your data is NOT automatically deleted when your license expires. To delete your data, you must submit a written deletion request (see Section 6.3).

6.3 User-Requested Deletion

You may request deletion of your data at any time by sending a written request to support@risingandthriving.com. Upon receiving your request, we will:

  1. Verify Your Identity: Confirm you are the account owner to prevent unauthorized deletion requests
  2. Process Within 30 Days: Delete your data from active systems within 30 business days
  3. Delete From:
    • Google Sheets (all transaction and financial data)
    • BigQuery (analytics and aggregated data)
    • Cloud Storage (uploaded documents and backups)
    • Email lists and support databases
  4. Retain For Legal Compliance: We may retain certain data as required by law (e.g., tax records, payment history) for up to 7 years

DELETION IS PERMANENT AND IRREVERSIBLE. Once deleted, your financial data cannot be recovered. We recommend downloading all reports and data before requesting deletion.
